## 📌 Executive Summary & Retention Problem

* **The Challenge:** AT Food Co. successfully acquires trial box users (50% off first box via No-Code MVP), but daily fresh dog food requires strict habit formation to prevent churn.
* **The Solution:** The **AT Food Co. Fresh Pass**—a recurring subscription program delivering weekly chef-prepared meal packs tailored to a dog's caloric needs, integrated with the *Dog Life Record* health app.
* **Core Objective:** Map a 5-stage growth funnel, write high-converting push notification triggers, and design a rigorous A/B testing framework to optimize subscriber retention and 6-month LTV.

---

## 🔄 5-Stage Fresh Pass Growth Funnel

### Funnel Flow
* **Stage 1: Awareness** → In-App Nudge Banner inside *Dog Life Record* app.
* **Stage 2: Consideration** → Caloric & Nutrition Customizer Quiz.
* **Stage 3: Conversion** → 50% Off Trial Checkout & Recurring Delivery Opt-in.
* **Stage 4: Retention** → Automated Weekly Delivery Management & Protein Swaps.
* **Stage 5: Advocacy** → Refer-a-Friend Loop ("Give ₹500, Get ₹500").

### Stage Breakdown & Execution Logic

1. **Awareness (In-App Nudges & Banners):**
   * *Mechanism:* Contextual banners inside the *Dog Life Record* app notifying trial users when their 7-day trial pack is 48 hours from running out.
   * *Primary Metric:* Banner Click-Through Rate (CTR %).

2. **Consideration (Caloric & Nutrition Customizer):**
   * *Mechanism:* Interactive nutrition quiz estimating exact daily caloric needs, portion sizes, and weekly savings vs. cooking at home.
   * *Primary Metric:* Quiz Completion Rate (%).

3. **Conversion (Trial to Subscription Checkout):**
   * *Mechanism:* Low-friction conversion checkout locking in recurring weekly home delivery with 1-click pause/skip flexibility.
   * *Primary Metric:* Trial-to-Paid Subscription Conversion Rate (CVR %).

4. **Retention (Habit Loop & Automated Care):**
   * *Mechanism:* Weekly pre-delivery SMS/Push reminders allowing pet parents to swap proteins (Beef, Chicken, Lamb) or adjust delivery times.
   * *Primary Metric:* 30-Day & 90-Day Retention Rate (%).

5. **Advocacy (Referral Flywheel):**
   * *Mechanism:* "Give ₹500, Get ₹500" referral loop triggered after a pet parent logs 14 consecutive days of fresh feeding in the app.
   * *Primary Metric:* Referral K-Factor & Viral Coefficient.

## 🧪 A/B Testing & Experimentation Framework

### Experiment Hypothesis
> *If we introduce personalized meal pack customization (protein swapping) during the checkout flow instead of a flat discount, then 30-day subscriber retention will increase by 15% because pet parents feel greater ownership over their dog's diet.*

### Test Setup & Parameters
* **Target Audience:** All new trial users completing their first 7-day fresh box.
* **Sample Split:** 50% Control (Standard Flat Discount Banner) vs. 50% Treatment (Interactive Meal Builder).
* **Primary KPI:** 30-Day Active Subscription Retention Rate (%).
* **Secondary KPI:** Average Order Value (AOV) and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

---

## 📈 Key Funnel Metrics & Unit Economics

* **Trial-to-Paid Conversion:** Benchmark target > 35% from trial box to recurring Fresh Pass.
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Blended CAC target across search ads and trial box discounts.
* **Monthly Churn Rate:** Capped at < 5% per month through automated delivery management in the app.
